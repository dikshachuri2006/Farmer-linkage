About FarmerLinkage

FarmerLinkage is a digital agricultural marketplace built to strengthen direct connections between farmers and commercial buyers. By removing intermediaries and providing transparent price discovery, we facilitate fair trade agreements, reliable transactions, and improved farmgate net realizations.

Platform Workflows

How It Works

Streamlined processes for agricultural producers and commercial buyers.

👨‍🌾

For Farmers

Grow, list, and sell produce with confidence
  1. 1 Register and verify your agricultural profile.
  2. 2 List your available produce lots with prices and location.
  3. 3 Receive, review, and accept competitive buyer offers.
  4. 4 Execute verified digital transactions and settlement receipts.
🏪

For Buyers

Procure quality crops directly at farmgate
  1. 1 Register and submit your commercial procurement profile.
  2. 2 Browse available farmgate produce listings nationwide.
  3. 3 Submit binding purchase offers directly to farmers.
  4. 4 Track agreement fulfillment and settlement receipts.
